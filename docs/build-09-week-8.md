# Build 09 Week 8

Week 8, "The Keystone," closes Phase 2. It turns the data-and-services strategy
from a declared direction into a governed data capability, while preserving the
dramatic irony that an aggressive data-rights posture may look strong at the
benchmark before it becomes dangerous later.

## Implemented

- Week 8 module with briefing, artifacts, advisor contexts, decision spec,
  deterministic scoring, and state updates
- registry and data migration for Week 8
- centralized Week 8 scoring, relationship, and keystone constants
- data-rights convergence from the Week 6 posture into Week 8 rights posture
- `land_grab` and `predictive_built` flag writes
- Ferraro and Tran relationship movement for channel and legal consequences
- Phase 2 benchmark generation after Week 8

## Foundation Refinements

Week 8 exposed two additive contract gaps:

- `through_lines.data_rights.posture` now accepts `shared_value` and `asserted`
  in addition to the existing postures.
- coherence drift event weights now accept `keystone` for the Week 8 midpoint
  audit.

No new state objects were added. The canonical data-rights field remains
`through_lines.data_rights.posture`.

## Benchmark Discipline

The Week 8 benchmark intentionally ranks visible performance only. Its
student-facing payload does not read or reveal:

- `through_lines.data_rights`
- `land_grab`
- `contested_aggressive`
- future Week 11 consequences

The benchmark score for Week 8 is accumulated score only. Hidden data-rights
risk remains internal state for later convergence.
