Week 9, "The Bet," opens Phase 3. It is the release-valve week: the board is
right to want AI, and the scoring rewards board-aligned substance rather than
penalizing alignment. The trap is AI theater.

## Release Valve

Week 9 deliberately suspends the earlier "board appetite is suspicious" pattern.
The board-aligned move, predictive maintenance on the proprietary data
foundation, scores well when the foundation exists. Theater scatter is penalized
because it fails the board's sound demand by substituting demo activity for
business value.

## Capability Dependence

A team without `predictive_built` can still make the right Week 9 strategic
choice, but the value is weaker because the Week 8 data foundation is missing.
That separates judgment from organizational capability.
